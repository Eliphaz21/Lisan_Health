# External Approval Gates

The following gates are deliberately not represented as completed by source code:

- Professional penetration test covering authentication, authorization, uploads, SSRF, injection, XSS, CSRF, and denial of service.
- Clinical safety review by qualified healthcare professionals.
- Ethiopian privacy and legal review, including data-protection obligations.
- Medical-device classification review before any commercial medical claim.
- Native-speaker review for Amharic, Afaan Oromoo, and Tigrinya text and speech.
- Backup/restore exercise for the chosen production database and storage.
- Dedicated AletCloud synthetic smoke test using the manually triggered workflow.

LisanHealth remains an educational explainer until these external gates are signed off.
